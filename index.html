        How to read the studio · decoding, structure, and anomalies

        Look for structure, then verify. Live reception converts HackRF IQ samples into spectra, statistical peaks, and alternate views. Replay uses saved IQ. Demo generates a synthetic scene. Each mode remains labeled. Frequency and gain changes reset the learned baseline; warm-up scores are unavailable. Anomaly scores describe spectral change, not the identity or origin of a signal.

        From radio waves to information. Detecting energy, estimating modulation, recovering symbols, parsing a protocol, and decrypting a payload are separate steps. This lab offers tentative AM/FM and bit interpretations plus CRC-checked decoding of its own ASDR-LAB frames. It cannot automatically decrypt arbitrary transmissions. This independent receive-only studio explores ordinary radio energy, statistical patterns, and known protocols. The 1.600 GHz preset is an editable receiver setting, not an identification of any signal’s origin.
